/server

The deal

Three moves. Everything else on the server exists because of them.

  • +1

    Land a kill

    One of their hearts moves to you, permanently. The cap is twenty — two full rows.

  • −1

    Die

    To a player, to lava, to gravity — doesn't matter. The floor is one heart. You never hit zero; you just lose everything else.

  • /withdraw

    Cash out

    Pull hearts out of yourself and carry them as items. Trade them, gamble them, or drop the lot when someone catches you.

No claims. Griefing is allowed.

If you can get into it, you can have it. Hide your base or build something you can defend. Staff don't roll back raids — that's the game.

Everything gets wiped eventually.

Not just the world — all of it. There's no schedule and no getting your stuff out first, so don't get too attached to a base. We post in Discord before it happens.

/rules

The short version

[Server] No cheating. No cheat clients, no macros, nothing playing the game for you. Bots get you banned across the whole network, not warned.

[Server] Nothing outside the game. Phishing links, account selling and real-money scams. Raid people all you like, but keep it in Minecraft.

[Server] 13 and over. You have to be 13 to play here. Anything involving minors gets reported, not moderated.

[Server] Don't stack alts. There's a cap on how many accounts you can run, and evading a ban doesn't reset anything.
