The server

BrickSMP

You start on 10 hearts. Kill a player and you take one of theirs, up to 20. Die to one and you lose one, down to a floor of 1. You can pull hearts out of yourself with /withdraw and carry them as items, which also means you can trade them, gamble them, or drop the lot when someone catches you.

There are no claims and griefing is allowed. If you can get into it, you can have it. Hide your base or build something you can defend. Staff don't roll back raids, that's the game.

Everything gets wiped eventually. Not just the world, all of it. There's no schedule and there's no getting your stuff out first, so don't get too attached to a base. We post in Discord before it happens.

Features

What's on it

The stuff you'll actually use once you're in.

  • Hearts

    /withdraw pulls hearts out of yourself as items. Carry them, trade them, or hand them over when someone has you cornered.

  • Bounties

    Put your own money on someone's head and let the server come after them. /bounties to set one or see who's worth hunting.

  • Teams

    Run with people instead of getting jumped by them. /team to make one or get invited into one.

  • Gems

    Earned just for playing, and spent in /gemshop on crate keys and ranks. Buying them is optional, playing gets you there too.

  • The shop

    Sell what you don't need, buy what you do. One currency across the whole server, and prices move because players move them.

  • Auction house

    Put your gear up and let people bid on it. Beats spamming prices in chat at 2am.

  • Trading

    Both inventories lock until you both hit confirm, so nobody runs off with your diamonds halfway through.

  • Vaults and spawners

    Storage you can get at from anywhere, and spawners that sit in a menu instead of a wall of blocks lagging your base.

  • Crates

    Keys drop for playing and for voting. Link your Discord to your account in game and you get one for that too.

  • Random teleport

    Drops you somewhere nobody has touched yet, for when everything near spawn is taken.

  • Homes and warps

    /sethome so you've got somewhere to run back to, and public warps for the places worth knowing about.

  • Anticheat

    Runs on the server, not your client. It catches killaura and reach without punishing you for having bad ping.

Rules

The short version

  • No cheating

    No cheat clients, no macros, nothing playing the game for you. Bots get you banned across the whole network, not warned.

  • Nothing outside the game

    Phishing links, account selling and real-money scams. Raid people all you like, but keep it in Minecraft.

  • 13 and over

    You have to be 13 to play here. Anything involving minors gets reported, not moderated.

  • Don't stack alts

    There's a cap on how many accounts you can run, and evading a ban doesn't reset anything.

The full list is in game under /rules and pinned in Discord. If that and this page ever disagree, go with the in-game one.
